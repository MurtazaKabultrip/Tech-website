Questions, answered

Anything else? Write to us — we reply within a day.

What kinds of websites do you build?

Any kind: company sites, online shops, booking systems, portfolios, web apps, SaaS platforms and mobile apps. If it runs in a browser or on a phone, we can build it.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the size and features. After a short call you get a fixed price per phase, in writing, so you always know the total before we start.

How long does it take?

A company website usually takes 2–3 weeks, an online shop 4–6 weeks. Larger web apps are built in phases with a working demo every week.

Can you fix or improve my existing website?

Yes. We fix errors, speed up slow sites, close security holes, redesign outdated pages and move old code to modern technology.

Will I be able to update the content myself?

Yes. You get an easy admin panel for texts, images, products and bookings — no coding needed.

What does the 1-year service guarantee cover?

For 12 months after launch we fix any bug in the work we delivered for free, keep the site secure and updated, and answer your questions. No small print.

Who owns the website?

You do. The design, the code and all accounts are yours from day one. No lock-in.
