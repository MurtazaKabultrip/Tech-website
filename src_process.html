The details

What working with us looks like, whether you're starting fresh or fixing something.

New websites and apps

We start with a call and a written proposal that splits the work into phases, each with its own fixed price. Nothing is built before you approve the design, and you get a working demo every week.

Fixing an existing website

Send us the link, the error and, if you have it, access to the code. We find the root cause, fix it and add an automated test so it can't quietly come back. You get a short written report.

Upgrades and redesigns

Outdated technology stops getting security updates. We modernise step by step behind automated tests, and plan the switch so your visitors never notice.

Payment and ownership

You pay per phase, by card or bank transfer. The design, the code and all accounts are yours from day one.
